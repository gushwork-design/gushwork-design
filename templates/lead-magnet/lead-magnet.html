Do this first, or your results will lie to you

ChatGPT adapts to whoever it’s talking to. Logged in, it leans on your past chats and already knows your business, so you see a version your buyers never get. Two rules keep the test honest: log out before you start, and don’t type your company name until Part 4. Name yourself earlier and ChatGPT starts working you into its answers, which hides the gap you’re trying to find.

Set up your browser

  • Use a private or incognito window for everything in this pack.
  • Log out of ChatGPT. Logged out, there’s no memory or saved history to skew your results, so you see what a real buyer sees.
  • Prefer to stay logged in? Turn on Temporary Chat and switch off memory in personalization settings instead. Logging out is simpler.

How to run each prompt

  • Copy a prompt, swap the [bracketed] parts for your details, and paste it in.
  • Check one thing: does your company get named? If not, write down who does.
  • Work through Parts 1 and 2 in ChatGPT alone, then bring in Perplexity and Gemini for Part 3, once you’ve seen what your buyer sees.

How the score works

Count how many of the ten prompts name you. That number, out of ten, is your visibility score, and it’s the one number that matters here. The lower it is, the more often AI is sending buyers to a competitor instead of you.

This isn’t a judgment of your company. A great supplier can score zero. It measures one thing: can AI find you when a buyer is deciding?

What does AI actually know about you?

Open a fresh chat, still logged out like the rest of the pack, and ask about your own company by name. Your buyers run these same searches. You’re checking one thing: does AI actually understand what your business does, or does it have you wrong?

17

Is anything wrong, outdated, or invented? Confidently wrong is worse than a blank, because your buyers read the same thing.

19
